# Jobs To Be Done

## J-1 (Table Payer): Tell everyone what they owe

When the bill arrives and my table has agreed to split it equally, I want to know exactly what each person owes, tip included, so I can ask each of them for the right amount straight away and settle up.

## J-2 (Table Payer): Settle on a tip

When the table is deciding how much to tip, I want to find out what each tip we are considering would come to in actual money, so I can agree on one that feels fair without guessing.

## J-3 (Table Payer): Share out the odd cents

When the amount we have to pay cannot be divided into equal amounts of whole cents, I want to know exactly how the extra cents are shared out, so I can collect exactly what I paid, with no arguments about odd cents and no shortfall for me to cover.

## J-4 (Table Payer): Redo the sums when things change

When something changes (the table changes its mind, someone joins or leaves, or I find I used a wrong figure) or another bill needs splitting, I want to redo the sums from the corrected figures or from scratch, so I can be sure nothing I tell anyone is based on a wrong or out-of-date figure.

## J-5 (Table Payer): Pay in cash without waiting for change

When I am paying the bill in cash, I want to hand over an amount I can make up from the notes and coins I have on me, so I can pay straight away without waiting for change.
